Inside Pack 03

Bring the demons to life.

A collection of twelve monsters, each with its own attacks, animation timing and dark fantasy identity.

  • Transparent spritesIndividual PNG frames and spritesheets, preserving the approved artwork and soft transparency.
  • Distinct movements and attacksGround and flying monsters, spectral dashes, blood magic, rage and expressive death sequences.
  • Unity and Unreal integrationsMonster demos, animation playback, timing and registration data, with English setup guides.
  • Pick the monsters you needDemons are offered individually on itch.io and Fab. Check each listing for its included actions, files and engine versions.
Technical details and compatibility

Each monster has its own animation contracts, timings, pivots and effects. Unity and Unreal downloads are provided through the individual listings; refer to their documentation for tested editor versions and render pipelines.

Animation previews demonstrate the included sprites. The artwork gallery is promotional illustration. PNGs and spritesheets contain the same poses in different arrangements. Integration demos are animation examples, rather than complete enemy AI or combat systems.
