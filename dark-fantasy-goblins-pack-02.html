Technical details and compatibility

Tested in Unity 6000.0.47f1 with the Built-in Render Pipeline. URP, HDRP and other editor versions are not claimed. PNGs and spritesheets can be adapted to other engines with the supplied timing and pivot manifests.

128 pixels per unit. Most character frames are 256 × 256; selected attacks use 320 × 256 or 256 × 320. Effects range from 48 × 96 to 192 × 192. Point filtering, transparency and consistent body roots are configured in Unity.

The editable scripts demonstrate animations and combat. Complete enemy AI, health, shield blocking, navigation, terrain following and boss phases are not included. Layered source artwork, generation projects and audio are not included.

AI-assisted artwork and production

OpenAI image generation was used for reference and separate effect artwork. AutoSprite was used for character animation frames. Codex assisted with example code, processing, documentation and validation.

The delivered content includes frame curation, user-directed pixel edits, alignment, timing, separate effects and Unity integration. This pack is not advertised as hand-drawn. No AI service or account is required to use the downloaded assets.
