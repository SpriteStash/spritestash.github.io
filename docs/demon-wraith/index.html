Demon Wraith — Dark Fantasy Bestiary / Pack 03

Sprite Stash. One floating demon monster and seven movements. The PNG download contains 178 production frame files and 13 spritesheets. This total includes 36 optional composed Attack2 frames; component and composed modes are alternative views of the same action. Sheets contain the same poses, not additional artwork.

Compatibility and validation

Tested in Unity 6000.0.47f1 / Built-in and Unreal Engine 5.8.3 / Paper 2D on Windows editors.

Other versions, URP/HDRP, UEFN, networking and packaged platforms are not validated. See the exact release's validation record and store compatibility fields. No C++ or Python runtime is required.

Open the demos

  • Unity: import the unitypackage into a Built-in project. Open Assets/SpriteStash/DemonWraith/Scenes/SC_DemonWraithDemo.unity and press Play.
  • Unreal: extract the ZIP and open DemonWraithDarkFantasyBestiaryPack03.uproject in the validated editor. Enable Paper 2D. Open Maps/L_DemonWraithOverview and press Play.
  • Keys 1-7 select Idle, Walk, Running, Attack2, Attack1, Hurt and Death. The preview sequence plays each once in that order, then repeats the whole presentation. Repetition resets the presentation origin; gameplay Attack1 does not reset its arrival.

Production timing

Idle: 16 frames, 1600 ms, loop. Walk: 16 frames, 1280 ms, loop. Running: 16 frames, 960 ms, loop. Attack1 and Attack2: 600 ms each, once, using 36 exact synchronized sample intervals of 16.6666666667 ms. Hurt: 9 frames, 520 ms, once. Death: 40 frames, 1600 ms, once and fully absent at its final frame. The complete presentation is 7160 ms with no extra holds.

Source manifests and Contracts specify actual per-frame timing. Display encoders may merge images or round preview delays; do not infer production counts or timing from encoded GIF/APNG frames. PNGs and sheets are the pixel authority. APNGs preserve soft alpha for Attack2 and Death; GIF palette/binary coverage is not a production texture format.

Registration and movement

This monster is airborne. Keep 256 pixels per art unit (Unity units), or 2.56 pixels per Unreal centimeter. Idle/Walk/Running/Hurt use 512 x 512 canvases and top-origin root (256,200). Attack bodies use 512 x 576 and (256,264). Death uses 896 x 800 and (576,382). Optional Attack2 composed frames use 1280 x 800 and (576,328). The root is a fixed art-registration anchor, not a terrestrial foot pivot. Never crop/recenter each pose by its visible bounds. Walk's six-degree and Running's ten-degree tilts are already baked into the pixels.

Attack1 moves the owning monster 240 pixels (0.9375 Unity units / 93.75 Unreal centimeters) between 183.333333 and 283.333333 ms, following the approved piecewise-linear curve. The destination persists after recovery, Hurt and Death. The body and claw follow the current owner; emitted afterimages keep their recorded world positions. Do not apply the root dash twice.

Attack2 keeps the owner fixed. The owner raises its hand with poses 1-5, retracts with 4-1, and returns to Idle at 450 ms. Only the pale soul dashes 240 pixels. The exact emergence, opacity and fade are in its contract. The default integrated playback uses component mode only. The optional composed sheet/Flipbook is an alternative; do not overlay it on the component playback. No independent soul collision/combat system is supplied.

Death uses the final composed PNGs only. Shadow, wind, rising soul, fade and disappearance are already combined. Never add another wind, shadow, soul, dissolve material or particle layer. Preserve fractional alpha and the blank final frame.

Unity integration

Drag Prefabs/PF_DemonWraith into your scene. DemonWraithPlayer and DemonWraithLibrary use six renderer slots and one exact clock. Call Play("Idle"), Play("Walk"), Play("Running"), Play("Attack1"), Play("Attack2"), Play("Hurt") or Play("Death"). Advance(milliseconds) advances production time; Evaluate(milliseconds) samples the current action. Auto-return recovers non-death one-shots to Idle and keeps Attack1's arrival. Attack2 recovery preserves its approved Idle exit phase. Set externalClock only when an external controller explicitly samples the player.

The reusable player owns its root translation; do not also move the owner with the same dash from your game controller. It uses ordinary Sprites/SpriteRenderers and a ScriptableObject library. It does not depend on an Animator, additional animation packages, custom shaders, physics or navigation. Original full-rectangle sprites use custom pivots, uncompressed RGBA, nearest filtering and no mipmaps. Keep the approved smoothing already baked into the pixels.

Unreal integration

Drag Blueprints/BP_DemonWraithPlayback into your level. Call PlayIdle, PlayWalk, PlayRunning, PlayAttack1, PlayAttack2, PlayHurt or PlayDeath. PlayState indices follow the demonstration order: 0 Idle, 1 Walk, 2 Running, 3 Attack2, 4 Attack1, 5 Hurt, 6 Death. UpdateAction(DeltaSeconds) advances the exact Blueprint clock. AutoPreview and DemoControls are off on the reusable Blueprint and enabled only on the demo actor. AutoReturnToIdle controls one-shot recovery; Death remains absent.

Draw0-Draw5 are render slots, ordered back to front. The Blueprint samples original PaperSprites with exact frame indices, alpha and world/current-owner positions. Its translucent unlit material preserves vertex opacity. Source PF_ Flipbooks permit independent source inspection; use the Blueprint for assembled attacks and persistent owner movement. Source inspection loops do not change gameplay one-shot contracts.

Documentation and migration

Open Documentation/L_WraithGuide in the Content Browser. Press Play or pilot its reading camera. Maps/L_WraithSourceAssets provides useful inspection of original animations, static trail/cast sources and all 13 sheets. Static sources are not assigned an invented independent loop. README.txt, LICENSE.txt and PLAYBACK.json are beside the guide on disk.

Free English guide: https://spritestash.github.io/docs/demon-wraith/

To migrate, enable Paper 2D in the destination project and use Asset Actions > Migrate on BP_DemonWraithPlayback, including dependencies. Preserve the product folder. Migrate the source gallery too if you want every original source sequence and sheet. Keep the validated editor version; do not assume backward compatibility.
