Winged Ravager — Dark Fantasy Bestiary / Pack 03

Sprite Stash. One airborne demon monster, five movements, 126 transparent animation PNGs, eight spritesheets and three separate flame effect sequences. Sheets repeat the same poses, not additional artwork.

Release validation

Tested in Unity 6000.0.47f1 / Built-in and Unreal Engine 5.8.3 / Paper 2D in Windows editors. Other engines, URP/HDRP, UEFN and packaged platforms are not validated. See the release validation record for the exact delivered version.

Downloads and setup

  • PNGs: production frames, eight exact-cell sheets, approved GIFs and portable timing/placement contracts.
  • Unity: import the unitypackage into a Built-in project. Open Assets/SpriteStash/WingedRavager/Scenes/SC_WingedRavagerDemo.unity and press Play.
  • Unreal: extract the ZIP, open WingedRavagerDarkFantasyBestiaryPack03.uproject, then Maps/L_WingedRavagerOverview and press Play. Paper 2D is required. No C++, Python or third-party runtime plugin is required.

Movements

Idle: 20 frames, 2000 ms, loop.

Fly: 20 frames, 1600 ms, loop.

Attack: 40 body frames, 4000 ms, once. Jet: six frames; MouthEmber: four; ThroatEmber: four. The complete action uses 80 synchronized samples of 50 ms.

Hurt: eight frames, 600 ms, once. Durations: 50, 70, 80, 70, 100, 80, 80, 70 ms.

Death: 24 frames, 1300 ms, once. Ash appears at 530 ms during the fall. The final frame is fully transparent at 1250 ms and is held for the actual remaining 50 ms.

Playback and registration

Keep the full 512 x 512 body canvas, root (288,288) from the upper left, and 256 pixels per art unit. This is an airborne root, not a foot anchor. One art unit equals one Unity unit or 100 Unreal centimeters (2.56 pixels/cm). All actions share this registration. No ground snap or recentering by changing visible bounds.

The approved composite is 1024 x 640, body at (32,64), actor origin (320,352). Fly's eight-degree tilt and Jet's five-degree tilt are already baked into their PNGs. Do not rotate again, stretch fire or add a connector/impact at the flame tip. Existing smoothing and manual pixel edits must be preserved.

Attack order, back to front: body, skin-clipped ThroatEmber, MouthEmber, Jet. Jet follows the tracked mouth plus (22,0); MouthEmber plus (12,0), uniform peak scale 0.51. Throat follows mouth plus (-20,-4), scale 0.60, clipped to the approved skin mask. The exact 80-sample track is in Contracts/Attack.json.

Engine Runtime textures are derived only by applying the approved scale, opacity and throat clipping recipe to source components at their recorded positions. They keep the source art unchanged and reproduce the synchronized layered playback. These derivatives are not additional artwork. The eight original sequences and sheets remain available separately. Use synchronized actions for the assembled attack; isolated effect previews do not define complete attack timing.

ApprovedGIFs are mandatory visual, timing and position references. The complete montage is 9500 ms, each action once. It repeats the entire presentation; this does not turn Attack, Hurt or Death into gameplay loops. Death-review.gif adds 600 ms of blank inspection time, excluded from runtime. Death ash is already in the PNGs: no second dissolve, corpse hold or added particles.

Unity integration

Drag Prefabs/PF_WingedRavager into your scene. WingedRavagerPlayer uses the supplied library and four renderers from one clock. Call Play("Idle"), Play("Fly"), Play("Attack"), Play("Hurt") or Play("Death"). Attack and Hurt return to Idle by default; Death remains absent. Disable autoReturnToIdle to inspect a one-shot end. Evaluate(milliseconds) samples action time; Advance(milliseconds) advances playback.

An alternative millisecond-keyed Animator controller is included. Enable Animator and set useAnimator=true when using the clips; never run both controllers together. Select the clips/states explicitly for your gameplay. Source sprites use full rectangles, custom pivots, uncompressed RGBA, nearest filtering and no mipmaps. Do not run another smoothing pass.

The demo has buttons and keys 1-5 for each movement. Preview all shows the approved sequence. The product supplies animation playback and effects, not navigation, combat, damage detection or enemy AI.

Unreal integration

Drag Blueprints/BP_WingedRavager into your level. Call PlayIdle, PlayFly, PlayAttack, PlayHurt or PlayDeath. PlayState accepts indices 0-4 in that order. AutoPreview and DemoControls are disabled on the reusable Blueprint and enabled on the overview actor. AutoReturnToIdle controls Attack/Hurt recovery. Death holds its blank final sprite.

BodyRenderer, ThroatRenderer, MouthRenderer and JetRenderer share the Blueprint Clock. Synchronized/PF_ assets contain the complete action duration and placement. Do not start a second automatic flipbook clock or change the relative layers. Animation source PF_ sequences can be inspected independently in the source gallery.

Documentation and reuse

Open Documentation/L_RavagerGuide in the Content Browser. Press Play or pilot its camera to read the native guide. Open Maps/L_RavagerSourceAssets to inspect all eight original sequences and their sheets. README.txt, LICENSE.txt and PLAYBACK.json are beside the native guide on disk; use your operating system to open text files.

Free English guide: https://spritestash.github.io/docs/winged-ravager/

To migrate, enable Paper 2D in the target project and use Asset Actions > Migrate on BP_WingedRavager, including dependencies. Preserve the product folder. Migrate the source gallery too when you want the original standalone sequences and sheets. Use an editor version validated for this release; do not assume backward compatibility.

Scope, credits and support

Animation assets, reusable playback, synchronized effects and demonstration scenes are included. AI, navigation, damage, a combat system, multiplayer and platform builds are outside the delivered scope.
