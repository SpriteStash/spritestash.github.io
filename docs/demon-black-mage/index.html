Demon Black Mage — Dark Fantasy Bestiary / Pack 03

Sprite Stash. One horned demon monster wielding a gemstone staff, with five movements and a living-shadow attack. Includes this monster only.

Included files and validation

400 transparent production PNGs and 14 original spritesheets. The total includes 95 optional composed frames (40 Attack and 55 Death); the remaining 305 files are body/effect components. Composed and component modes are alternatives, not extra unique artwork. Sheets repeat the same poses.

Tested in Unity 6000.0.47f1 / Built-in and Unreal Engine 5.8.3 / Paper 2D on Windows editors.

Other engine versions, URP/HDRP, UEFN, networking and packaged platform builds are not validated. No C++ or Python runtime is required.

Open the demos

Unity: import the unitypackage into a Built-in project. Open Assets/SpriteStash/DemonBlackMage/Scenes/SC_DemonBlackMageDemo.unity and press Play.

Unreal: extract to a short path and open DemonBlackMageDarkFantasyBestiaryPack03.uproject in the validated engine. Enable Paper 2D. Open Maps/L_DemonBlackMageOverview and press Play.

Keys 1-5 select Idle, Walk, Attack, Hurt and Death. Preview all plays each movement once, then repeats the 6600 ms presentation. That review loop is separate from gameplay loops.

Timing and registration

Idle: 12 frames, 1200 ms, loop. Walk: 14 frames, 1400 ms, loop. Attack: 16 body frames and synchronized effects, 1600 ms, once. Hurt: 6 frames, 400 ms (40,80,70,70,60,80), once. Death: 55 synchronized samples, 2000 ms, once until complete absence. Attack/Hurt recover to Idle; Death remains absent. Source inspection loops do not change runtime one-shot behavior.

Preserve every sample duration in PLAYBACK.json and the production contracts, including Death's short 10/30 ms intervals. No review-only hold is added. APNG preserves the approved soft alpha; GIF's ordered transparency is only a preview limitation.

Use 256 pixels per Unity unit or 2.56 pixels per Unreal centimeter. Body canvases are 512 x 512; fixed top-origin foot root (256,426), normalized bottom-left pivot (0.5,0.16796875). Do not recenter by changing visible bounds. The actor root stays fixed in all actions; walking is in place. Frame sizes range from 160 x 160 to 1280 x 800. Source sheets reach 10240 x 4000 (Attack composed); the engines use 15 native atlas pages, splitting that sheet into two pages without resampling. The PNG download retains all 14 original sheets unchanged.

Attack: synchronized living shadow

Default runtime uses five component slots: Character, Ground, Charge, Transfer and GemWisp, in contract layer order. All placements and alpha multipliers come from the exact 40 sample intervals. Charge and Transfer already contain their approved 90 percent alpha: do not multiply it again. Ground uses the contract 90 percent multiplier. GemWisp uses 95 percent and the sampled fades; it is 160 x 160, pivot (80,80), offset (-8,-6) from the gemstone, active 560-1360 ms. Keep the original accepted gemstone lighting.

The shadow travels from gemstone through the body to the ground. The staff sways once and starts lowering at 1120 ms as the ground effect retracts. Ground appears at 720 ms; the approved reverse uses the same source frames. Never stretch, relocate or independently retime the layers. The Attack/Composed frames are an optional baked alternative; never draw them on top of component playback.

Death: sinking shadow pool

Default runtime draws PoolBack, SinkingBody, InwardDust and PoolFront synchronously. The pool begins at 120 ms with kneeling. Sinking, staff movement, shade, erosion and the pool's 95 percent opacity are already baked into these PNGs. Keep the actor root fixed. Do not translate the body again, scale it down, add another dissolve or add another pool. Fine dust pulls inward/downward; no upward wind is added. The final frame is empty. Death/Composed is an alternative to the four tracks, never an additional layer.

Unity integration

Drag Prefabs/PF_DemonBlackMage into your scene. DemonBlackMagePlayer and DemonBlackMageLibrary use five SpriteRenderers and one exact clock. Call Play("Idle"), Play("Walk"), Play("Attack"), Play("Hurt") or Play("Death"). Advance(milliseconds) advances production time; Evaluate(milliseconds) samples it. autoReturnToIdle recovers Attack/Hurt and carries excess elapsed time into Idle. externalClock is for explicit sampling by an external controller. The reusable prefab does not need the demo script.

This uses ordinary Sprites/SpriteRenderers and a ScriptableObject library, not Animator clips. Keep custom pivots, uncompressed RGBA, Point filtering, no mipmaps and the approved smoothing already in the source. No extra texture smoothing or shader dissolve is needed.

Unreal integration

Drag Blueprints/BP_DemonBlackMagePlayback into your level. Call PlayIdle, PlayWalk, PlayAttack, PlayHurt or PlayDeath. PlayState indices 0-4 follow that order. UpdateAction(DeltaSeconds) advances the exact Blueprint clock. AutoPreview and DemoControls are off on the reusable Blueprint and on only for the demo. AutoReturnToIdle recovers Attack/Hurt. Draw0-Draw4 use the original PaperSprites, sampled positions, alpha and layer order. The translucent unlit material preserves vertex opacity. No C++ or Python runtime is required.

The 14 source PF_ Flipbooks allow independent inspection of the production sources, not an extra animation/combat system. Use the Blueprint for assembled actions. For sources without a standalone cycle, inspection keys follow the contract usage sequence; the showcase loops are not production behavior.

Documentation, migration and modification

Open Documentation/L_BlackMageGuide in the Content Browser. Press Play or pilot its reading camera. Maps/L_BlackMageSourceAssets displays the 14 source groups and 15 engine atlas pages. README.txt, LICENSE.txt and PLAYBACK.json sit beside the guide on disk.

Enable Paper 2D in the destination Unreal project. Asset Actions > Migrate on BP_DemonBlackMagePlayback, including dependencies. Preserve the product folder. Migrate the source gallery separately to include all optional composed sequences and atlas pages. Unity imports under Assets/SpriteStash/DemonBlackMage. Modify controller logic while retaining original pixels, pivots, timing and layer contracts.
