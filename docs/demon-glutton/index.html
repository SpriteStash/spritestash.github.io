Demon Glutton — Dark Fantasy Bestiary / Pack 03

Sprite Stash. One animated demon monster with 6 movements, 129 transparent animation PNGs, 11 spritesheets, and 5 separate effect sequences. Sheets arrange the same poses, not additional artwork.

Validated editors

Unity 6000.0.47f1 / Built-in and Unreal Engine 5.8.3 / Paper 2D, in Windows editors. Other engine versions, URP/HDRP, UEFN, packaged platforms, and multiplayer replication are not validated.

Downloads

  • PNGs: full-canvas production frames, exact-cell spritesheets, timing/pivot manifests, action contracts, and approved preview GIFs.
  • Unity: import the .unitypackage into a Built-in project. Open Assets/SpriteStash/DemonGlutton/Scenes/SC_DemonGluttonDemo.unity, then press Play.
  • Unreal: extract the project, open DemonGluttonDarkFantasyBestiaryPack03.uproject, and open Content/DemonGluttonDarkFantasyBestiaryPack03/Maps/L_DemonGluttonOverview. Paper 2D is required. Press Play.

Movements

Idle: 11 frames / 1100 ms / loop.

Walk: 21 frames / 2100 ms / loop.

Attack1: 22 body frames / 1520 ms / once. Separate 7-frame club trail and 7-frame ground impact, behind the body.

Hurt: 8 frames / 540 ms / once.

Attack2: 22 body frames / 1780 ms / once. Separate 10-frame stream and 7-frame splash source sequences. The synchronized action repeats their sustain sections; do not substitute an isolated FX duration for the attack timeline.

Death: 7 body poses / 1510 ms complete / once. Two red warning flashes, then a 7-frame explosion. The body remains until the 980 ms peak and stays absent afterwards.

Playback and placement

All production components use 256 pixels per art unit. One art unit is one Unity unit or 100 Unreal centimeters (2.56 pixels/cm). Keep the complete canvases, custom pivots, original alpha, and physical scale. Do not trim, resmooth, recenter by bounds, or reapply historical mirror/rotation corrections.

Main body canvases are 512 x 512. Attack1 uses a padded 704 x 640 canvas. Ground effects use 512 x 576; the death explosion uses 1024 x 1024. Exact registration and per-frame timing are in the manifests. Effects include transparent terminal cells.

ApprovedGIFs are visual references. Review GIFs contain extra presentation pauses; game playback excludes these pauses. The action montage runs 8550 ms. Separate Idle and Walk remain loops.

Unity integration

Use Prefabs/PF_DemonGlutton with DemonGluttonPlayer and its serialized library. Call Play("Idle"), Play("Walk"), Play("Attack1"), Play("Hurt"), Play("Attack2"), or Play("Death"). The component drives body, effect, and contact renderers from the exact synchronized timeline. Attack/Hurt recover to Idle by default; the demo's automatic montage plays each action once.

The included Animator controller and millisecond-keyed clips are an alternative. To use it, enable the prefab Animator and set DemonGluttonPlayer.useAnimator to true so the two playback systems do not compete. Death clip events reset and fire the same single damage pulse.

For real blood contact, set groundLayers to your ground collision layer, or call SetBloodContact(worldPosition) during Attack2. The splash stays at that contact for this attack; the approved stream is not rescaled into a projectile. With no contact layer configured, the approved preview contact is used.

Death damage is an example integration: IGluttonDamageReceiver receives one configurable hit per target at 980 ms. damageLayers filters targets. deathPulse is an optional event. damageAmount defaults to 10 for demonstration, not a prescribed balance value. The suggested art-derived radius is 310 pixels / 1.2109375 units; the center is 16 pixels right and 150 pixels above the root. Adapt damage, target categories, and obstacle rules to your game.

Unreal integration

Use Blueprints/BP_DemonGlutton. PlayState accepts 0 Idle, 1 Walk, 2 Attack1, 3 Hurt, 4 Attack2, 5 Death; named Play functions are also provided. Demo controls use keys 1–6. AutoPreview and DemoControls are disabled on the reusable Blueprint and enabled on the overview actor.

Raw PF_ flipbooks preserve source sequence timings. Synchronized PF_ flipbooks and registered sprites retain the approved relative placement, sustained blood FX, body hold, and terminal transparency. The Blueprint uses one clock to sample all three renderers. Do not enable a second autonomous playback clock on those components.

SetBloodContact(Point) overrides blood ground contact. The Blueprint also performs an attack-start visibility trace from the mouth toward the approved ground sample; provide appropriate blocking ground geometry. It does not create a free-flying projectile.

At 980 ms, UpdateDeathDamage calls native ApplyRadialDamage once per death action. DamageAmount is configurable; the radius and center match the art-derived Unity example. Native collision and damage-prevention rules apply. Implement your game's damage receivers and balance. The remaining droplets do not trigger more pulses.

Scope

Animation assets, reusable playback, effect synchronization, and integration examples are included. Enemy AI, navigation, a complete combat system, multiplayer replication, and packaged platform builds are outside the delivered scope. Do not assume support for untested engines, render pipelines, or platforms. Native validation results are recorded separately for this release.
