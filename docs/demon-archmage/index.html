Demon Archmage — Dark Fantasy Bestiary / Pack 03

One grounded blood-mage boss. Seven actions: Idle, Walk, Attack1 Living Blood Orb, Attack2 Blood Claw, Attack3 Blood Arrow, Hurt, Death. No Running action. Original art, manual cutouts, thin contour and soft RGBA alpha are preserved.

Quick start — Unity

Import the unitypackage into a Built-in project. Content is under Assets/SpriteStash/DemonArchmage. Open Scenes/SC_DemonArchmageDemo and press Play. Buttons select actions; All actions plays the approved 13100 ms showcase. Drag Prefabs/PF_DemonArchmage into your scene for reusable playback without the automatic showcase.

Call DemonArchmagePlayer.Play with an exact action name. externalClock=false uses Update; externalClock=true accepts Advance(milliseconds). Evaluate(milliseconds) samples a state without advancing gameplay. autoReturnToIdle controls ordinary recovery. Death stays absent.

Quick start — Unreal

Extract the ZIP, open DemonArchmageDarkFantasyBestiaryPack03.uproject and enable Paper2D. Open Maps/L_DemonArchmageOverview and press Play. Keys 1–7 select Idle, Walk, Attack1, Attack2, Attack3, Hurt, Death. The automatic showcase is a presentation only. Open Documentation/L_DemonArchmageGuide for the in-editor guide, and Maps/L_DemonArchmageSourceAssets for the source gallery.

Drag Blueprints/BP_DemonArchmageController into your level. Named Play functions or PlayState 0–6 select actions. AutoPreview and DemoControls default off; AutoReturnToIdle defaults on. Use Asset Actions > Migrate on the controller and include dependencies. Keep the product folder. Migrate the source gallery too if its optional views are desired.

Exact timing and registration

Idle and Walk: body 12 frames ×100 ms = 1200 ms; combined body/contour loop 2400 ms (48 ×50 ms). The showcase displays only 1200 ms of each. Attack1: 4680 ms demonstration, caster gesture 1340 ms. Attack2: 880 ms. Attack3: 2220 ms. Hurt: 360 ms (40/60/60/60/60/80). Death: 2560 ms once. No 600 ms inspection holds are included in the game.

Use 256 pixels per Unity unit, 2.56 pixels per Unreal centimeter,physical scale 1. Every trimmed sprite stores an original crop and registration. Never recenter by changing bounds, weapon or wings. PNG top-left offsets become(dx/256,-dy/256) in Unity and(dx/2.56,0,-dy/2.56) in Unreal. Do not resmooth, repaint, stretch, rerotate or restore manually removed pixels.

Synchronized blood attacks

The normal Play actions use the exact approved Composed presentation. All gains, recoil, collection, contour, claw rotation and ash are already evaluated. White tint, alpha 1. Components OR Composed: never render both. Shared keys are optional alternatives.

Attack1: orb forms 400–620 ms and launches 840 ms. The caster returns to animated Idle at 1340 ms. RecoveryIdle preserves the approved contour phase in a2400 ms loop, instead of resetting its animation. Call PlayAttack1Gameplay to detach the orb from the caster; the caster becomes available at 1340 ms while the orb keeps travelling. The orb uses the approved local 1800 ms loop, 400 px/s flight, 11 px vertical hovering, 1100 ms hover period and 300 ms core spin. Local orb alpha 95% is applied once; evaluated formation already has its gain. The first 160 ms ramps the hover amplitude, as recorded in the approved authoring recipe. The orb remains independent when the owner moves or changes action. Call StopOrb from your game for collision/range/lifetime removal. The 4680 ms showcase edge exit is not a gameplay lifetime. This sample provides a single orb slot: spawn separate actor/prefab instances in your own system for simultaneous projectiles. No invented impact or return-to-caster effect.

Attack2: formation 260–420 ms,stroke 420–560,reabsorption 560–800,total 880 ms. Tilt 15 degrees and front/rear masks are baked. No extra rotation or gain.

Attack3: charge 540–1140 ms,launch 1140,flight ends 2100,action ends 2220. Arrow 292 px, 92 ms local spin; demonstrated travel 1400 px,progressive breakup,no target impact. Recoil,flash and pressure pulse are baked. Gameplay target/damage/lifetime logic belongs to your game.

Death and contour

Death kneels with the staff for 960 ms then disperses into natural ash for 1600 ms. Body, wings, staff and contour disappear together. It ends absent and never automatically returns to Idle. Do not add a second dissolve. The thin contour is the only persistent effect; no old blood whips, pools or frontal aura. Evaluated layer opacity is already baked.

Delivery contents and formats

The raw-art download contains 3095 animation PNG files and 246 original sheet pages, including evaluated layers, Composed alternatives and local Shared keys. These are alternative representations and repeated timeline samples, not 3095 unique body poses. PNG and atlas cells contain the same artwork. All original production pixels remain unchanged. Original playback.json, PIVOTS-AND-PLACEMENT.json and Contracts contain numeric source authority; ENGINE-PLAYBACK.json describes the integrated native banks. Full authoring previews/history are not required for importing production frames.

Native delivery uses 18 lossless 4096px atlas pages with recorded trimmed RGBA regions. Crops reduce storage without changing visible pixels or registration. Unity uses uncompressed full-rectangle sprites; Unreal uses native Paper2D sprites and flipbooks with exact millisecond schedules. Source metadata engine_tested=false records the art handoff, not an engine validation claim.

Compatibility and scope

Validated editors: Unity 6000.0.85f2 Built-in and Unreal Engine 5.8.3 Paper2D on Windows. Final distribution archives are validated in clean projects before release. Other versions, URP/HDRP, UEFN and packaged platforms are not validated. This is art and playback integration, not enemy AI, complete combat, damage application, gameplay collisions or audio.
