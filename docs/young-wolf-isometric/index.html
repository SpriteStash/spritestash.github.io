Young Wolf Isometric

One adolescent wolf for isometric games, with eight directions and seven animation states. The package includes 752 body PNGs and 80 Bite layer PNGs. The same frames are arranged in 56 body sheets and 16 Bite sheets.

Unity

  • Import the package into a Unity project, then open Assets/SpriteStash/YoungWolfIsometric/Scenes/SC_YoungWolfOverview and enter Play Mode. Use the on-screen controls to choose a direction and action.
  • Add Prefabs/PF_YoungWolfIsometric to your scene. The Animator uses action and direction names such as Idle2_SE and Attack_N. Play a state at normalized time zero to restart it.
  • The demo uses 384 raster pixels per Unity unit. Keep the local registration of the three SpriteRenderers when changing the size or integrating the prefab.
  • Tested in the Windows Editor with Unity 6000.0.85f2 and the Built-in Render Pipeline.

Unreal Engine

  • Open the project in Unreal Engine 5.8.3 with Paper2D enabled. Open Documentation/L_YoungWolfGuide in the product folder for the in-editor guide and Maps/L_YoungWolfOverview for the playable demo.
  • Press Play. Keys 1–7 select Idle, Idle2, Walk, Running, Attack, Hurt and Death. The numpad selects the facing. Open Maps/L_YoungWolfAnimationOverview to compare all 56 directional sequences.
  • Enable Paper2D in the destination project and migrate Blueprints/BP_YoungWolfIsometric with its dependencies. Use PlayState with action indices 0–6 and facing indices 0–7 in the order N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW.
  • The demo uses 3.84 pixels per Unreal centimeter. The PaperSprite pivots encode the raster placement of the animation keys. Tested in the Windows Editor with Unreal Engine 5.8.3.

Source artwork and timing

  • Body cells are 384 × 384 px. Bite cells are 256 × 256 px. Sheets use five columns and fill from left to right, then top to bottom. Keep the full frame canvas and skip the unused cells at the end of a sheet.
  • Consult ANIMATIONS.json in the art archive for the action and direction manifests. Each animation manifest provides frame order and durations in milliseconds. The native packages include the corresponding Documentation/PLAYBACK.json.
  • Idle and Walk loop at 1,200 ms and 2,100 ms. Idle2 and Running loop at the durations specified for each direction.
  • Attack, Hurt and Death play once at 580 ms, 450 ms and 980 ms respectively. Death holds the final downed pose. The promotional loop uses separate presentation holds.

Bite placement

  • Apply the recorded visual body advance once. The endpoint is approximately 18 px along the chosen direction. The effect origins already include that advance and are measured from the top left of the unshifted body canvas.
  • Draw the rear effect, then the body, then the front effect. The effect is active from 160 ms until 340 ms. The visual closure is at 250 ms. Use the baked alpha of the front layer. The South direction uses the front layer for the whole effect.
  • The raster center at (192, 192) is the alignment reference. Set your gameplay scale, ground anchor, movement transfer, collisions and hit timing in your project. The demos provide visual playback.
